Boilers Zhytomyr series are designed for heating rooms equipped with a water heating system with natural or forced circulation of the coolant.

The combustion products are discharged into the chimney.

.

The boiler is designed to operate on solid fuel (calculated fuel is anthracite (JSC GOST 8188 class 25-50 mm).
As an alternative fuel, hard and brown coal, firewood and briquetted solid fuel can be used.

The Zhytomyr boiler is equipped with a unique bimetallic heat exchanger, which improves heat transfer and boiler efficiency, as well as reliably protects the boiler from rust and extends the life of the boiler!

Now, when you buy an ATEM solid fuel boiler, you get the opportunity to make it electric at any time!

The Zhytomyr boilers provide a place for installing a heating element, which you can purchase at a store in your city.

Boilers Atem are compatible with heating elements tm «Tenko», or others that match the installation dimensions. Read more about installing an electric heating element in the instruction manual.

On the top cover of the boiler there is a thermal indicator to control the temperature of the boiler.

On the front side of the boiler there are three doors, on the other sides it is closed with a decorative lining. The upper door serves to load fuel into the combustion chamber and to clean the convective gas duct of the heat exchanger from soot. The lower door serves to ignite the fuel, maintain the grates and the ash chamber, in which the ash box is located.

There is also an air damper with an adjusting screw on the lower door, which is used to change the volume of air supplied to the boiler furnace.
