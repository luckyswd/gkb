Parapet-type gas boilers of the Zhytomyr-M series with the removal of combustion products through the outer wall of the building are the best solution for heating rooms that are not equipped with a chimney channel. The range of boilers «Zhytomyr-M» consists of five models with a capacity of 5, 7, 10, 12.5 and 16 kW

Sealed combustion chamber.

Boilers «Zhitomir-M» have a sealed combustion chamber, which ensures high safety of operation of the boiler, and allows you to install it in multi-storey apartment buildings. The intake of fresh air for the operation of the boiler and the removal of flue gases occur through the outer wall of the building using a smoke-air unit, which is supplied with the boiler.

 

All models are equipped with automatic equipment 630 EUROSIT (Italy), which has a built-in gas reducer, piezo ignition, flame sensor and overheat sensor, and POLIDORO burners allow achieving the most efficient gas combustion, and, accordingly, high efficiency and economy. Each produced boiler is subjected to seven types of reliability tests, during which it is tested in operating and extreme conditions.
