Super efficient on gas and consistently reliable on solid fuel!

The gas block of the Zhitomir-9 boiler is built on the basis of the efficient and very popular Zhitomir-3 model, which has a very high efficiency of up to 94%, which is a very high indicator belonging to the premium segment boilers!

The design of the Atem heat exchanger provides for two modes of operation — calm and turbulent.

In the «quiet» mode, with relatively warm weather outside, hot gases smoothly pass all the way through the heat exchanger, managing to give off heat to the heating system.

When the temperature outside starts to drop lower, the draft in the chimney increases significantly, which leads to an increase in the rate of extraction of hot flue gases and then the heat exchanger goes into a “turbulent” mode of operation, in which hot gases begin to actively mix, providing efficient heat transfer to the heating system!

While in boilers with a conventional heat exchanger, with an increase in thrust, the efficiency can drop sharply, the efficiency of an ATEM boiler with a turbulent heat exchanger remains very high in any mode of operation!

Such a constructive solution made it possible to reduce gas consumption, especially at low external temperatures, when the thrust increases and the air flow speed increases.
