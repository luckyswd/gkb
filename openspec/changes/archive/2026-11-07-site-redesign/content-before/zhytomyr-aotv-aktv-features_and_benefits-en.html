The overall dimensions of the furnace of the Zhytomyr boiler allow you to comfortably load fuel of various sizes, and the design of the heat exchanger is designed in such a way as to significantly reduce the clogging of the boiler with combustion products and ensure stable operation in any conditions!

For convenient operation, AKTV models are equipped with a cooking surface for cooking.

Boilers are produced with a capacity of 12, 14, 18 and 22 kW.

Steel thickness up to 6 mm.

 

ADVANTAGES OF BOILERS ZHYTOMYR

  • Powder coating, the estimated life of the equipment with proper operation and timely maintenance is up to 20 years.

  • Hob with cast iron circles for cooking. When not in use, it closes with a decorative lid with thermal insulation.

  • The three-way heat exchanger maximizes heat transfer, slows down the passage of flue gases and increases the efficiency of the boiler (up to 90%).
  • The boiler is made of steel 4-6 mm thick.
  • Electric reserve! Zhytomyr boilers are prepared for the installation of an electric heating element, both with and without a control unit. With the help of an electric heating element, you can maintain the temperature in the heating system when you do not have a home or do not have the opportunity to load firewood. The house will be warm and the heating system will not freeze. In addition, it is possible to use such a scenario when the solid fuel has already burned out, the temperature begins to decrease and the electric heating element automatically turns on automatically and maintains the set temperature until the next ignition of the boiler.
  • Pig-iron reliable grid-irons.

  • Boiler door with thermal insulation.

  • Decorative cladding with an air gap ensures a comfortable temperature of the boiler surface and protects against burns.
    Possibility of installing both a mechanical thermostat and a forced electronically controlled booster.
