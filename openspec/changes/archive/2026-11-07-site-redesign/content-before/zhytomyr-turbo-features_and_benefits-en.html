«Zhitomir-Turbo» is a series of highly efficient gas boilers with forced removal of combustion products with a capacity of 10 to 40 kW.

.

 

Stable work in any conditions!

Thanks to the forced removal of combustion products (turbine), the Zhytomyr-Turbo boiler works clearly and stably in any conditions.

 

 

.

Always high efficiency!

The efficiency of the boiler is always at around 93-94% and does not depend on the draft in the chimney and other factors.

The use of forced flue gas removal in the boiler made it possible to equip the boiler with a heat exchanger with enhanced heat extraction and turbulators with an increased working area. This ensures efficient heat exchange and high heat output with a fairly compact size of the boiler! .

 

High level gas valve!

«Zhytomyr-Turbo» is equipped with a gas valve SIT 820 NOVA, which ensures stable and economical operation of the boiler, and also allows you to connect a room thermostat to the boiler! This allows you to more accurately adjust the operating mode of the boiler and significantly reduce gas consumption!

 

.

Maximum security!

The Zhytomyr-Turbo boiler is equipped with additional safety devices, such as an overheating (boiling) sensor and a pressure manostat that turns off the gas supply when the turbine stops or there is no electricity.
