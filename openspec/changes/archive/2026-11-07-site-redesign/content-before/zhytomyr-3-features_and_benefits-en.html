Turbulent heat exchanger «ATEM» — maximum heat!

 

The process of transferring heat from gas combustion to the heating system occurs precisely in the boiler heat exchanger, therefore, how it copes with this task directly affects the gas consumption and the efficiency of the boiler.

In boilers of an outdated design, developed when no one thought about saving yet, the heat exchangers are built in such a way that half of the heat simply flies out into the chimney practically without lingering in the boiler.

Unlike older design boilers, the Atem boiler heat exchanger is built in such a way as to provide maximum efficiency combined with reduced gas consumption!

The efficiency of Zhytomyr-3 boilers reaches 94%, which is a very high indicator inherent in premium segment boilers!

 

 

The design of the Atem heat exchanger provides for two modes of operation — calm and turbulent.

In «quiet» mode, with relatively warm weather outside, hot gases smoothly pass all the way through the heat exchanger, having time to give off heat to the heating system.

When the temperature outside starts to drop lower, the draft in the chimney increases significantly, which leads to an increase in the rate of hot flue gases, and then the heat exchanger goes into a «turbulent» mode of operation, in which hot gases begin to actively mix, providing efficient heat transfer to the heating system!

While in boilers with a conventional heat exchanger, with an increase in thrust, the efficiency can drop sharply, the efficiency of an ATEM boiler with a turbulent heat exchanger remains very high in any mode of operation!

Gas duct with a floating damper — saves heat!

 

Unlike simple «open» draft compensators, which are still used in boilers of many brands, Atem boilers are equipped with a proprietary floating flue damper, which, on the one hand, prevents the loss of warm air from the room, and on the other, ensures stable operation of boiler safety sensors.

 

 

Such a constructive solution made it possible to reduce gas consumption, especially at low external temperatures, when the thrust increases and the air flow speed increases.

 

 

«ATEM START» — easy start in any conditions! (models 10 kW, 12.5 kW)

 

Any chimney boiler can be difficult to start after a long stop in operation, when the chimney has time to cool down. Also, starting is complicated in adverse weather conditions or simply with a not very high-quality chimney.

«Atem Start» system helps to solve this problem!

 

 

When starting the boiler, the «Atem Start» system allows you to increase the temperature of the flue gases in the chimney in order to «break through» the air lock and quickly warm up the chimney to stabilize the draft.

The Atem Start system is equipped with models with a capacity of 10 and 12.5 kW.

 

 

«Atem Electroreserve» — make your boiler electric! (models 10-16 kW)

Now, buying a gas or solid fuel boiler «ATEM», you get the opportunity at any time to make it also electric!

In boilers «Zhytomyr-3» with a power of 10 to 16 kW, a heating element installation site is provided, which you can purchase at a store in your city.

Boilers Atem are compatible with heating elements tm «Tenko», or others that match the mounting dimensions. Read more about installing an electric heating element in the instruction manual.

 

 

«Atem Protect» — the boiler will last longer!

 

Molten aluminum is sprayed onto the inside of the furnace and the lower part of the chimney heat exchanger of ATEM gas boilers, which, when in contact with the heated steel elements of the boiler, forms an interalloy, which in its properties is very close to stainless steel.

This reliably protects the boiler from corrosion even under the most unfavorable operating conditions and significantly increases the life of the boiler!
