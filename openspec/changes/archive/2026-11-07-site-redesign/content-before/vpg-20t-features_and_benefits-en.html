Functions and features of the water heater ATEM VPG-20T:

  • The water heater uses water-controlled automatic ignition and ionic flame control, which make the use of the water heater easy and convenient.
  • Flame failure protection:  ion flame control will automatically shut off the gas supply when the burner flame goes out, which completely eliminates gas leakage.
  • Low water pressure:  The water heater works even if the water pressure in the system is 0.01MPa!, which means it is suitable for users who live on higher floors or have low water pressure in the pipeline.
  • Protection against «dry» combustion:  when the water heater is turned on without water flow, the protection device will automatically turn off the gas supply.
  • High pressure protection:  once the water pressure exceeds 1MPa, the safety valve will automatically reduce the pressure to avoid damage.
  • Traction control:  if the turbine stops working and the sensor detects no air flow, the air pressure switch will immediately turn off the gas supply.
  • Frost protection:  This machine is equipped with an electrical frost protection system that allows the machine to operate smoothly at ambient temperatures down to -40°C.
  • Overheating protection:  if the water temperature exceeds the temperature of the overheating sensor (90°C), the gas supply will be immediately cut off and the water heating will stop. It is possible to restore the operation of the device in 5-10 minutes after the overheating sensor has cooled down.
  • Rational use of water:  after 20 minutes of using hot water, the water heater will automatically turn off.
  • If the permissible current  or voltage is exceeded, the circuit breaker will stop supplying power to the device.
