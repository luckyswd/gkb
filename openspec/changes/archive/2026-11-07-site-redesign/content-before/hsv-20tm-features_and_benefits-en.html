Functions and features:

  • Protection:  ion flame control will automatically shut off the gas supply when the burner flame goes out, which completely eliminates gas leakage.
  • Energy Saving:  The machine uses an advanced gas unit that will allow you to save gas and use the water heater more economically.
  • Low water pressure operation : The water heater works even if the water pressure in the system is 0.01 MPa (0.1 bar), which is suitable for users who live on higher floors or have low water pressure in the pipeline.
  • Dry burning protection:  when the water heater is turned on without water flow, the protection device will automatically turn off the gas supply.
  • High pressure protection:  once the water pressure exceeds 1MPa, the safety valve will automatically reduce the pressure to avoid damage.
  • Overheating protection : when the water in the machine is heated above 75 ° C, the overheat sensor will work and stop the gas supply to the burner. After 20 minutes of continuous operation, the device automatically turns off, to continue working, the device must be restarted.
  • Frost protection:  the anti-freeze system will prevent the unit from failing when installed in an unheated room.
  • Draft control:  if the draft in the chimney is insufficient and there is a danger of combustion products entering the room, the draft control device will immediately turn off the gas supply.
  • Flame modulation:  the flame height is automatically adjusted to the required conditions, which allows you to optimize the operation of the device and reduce gas consumption.
  • Digital display with touch control  and low battery indicator provides simple and convenient operation of the device.
